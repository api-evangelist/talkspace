This article examines the connection between perimenopause and mental health challenges including depression, anxiety, and mood fluctuations driven by hormonal shifts. The piece explores how changing estrogen and progesterone levels during the perimenopausal transition affect brain chemistry and emotional wellbeing. It also discusses treatment options and the role of therapy in supporting mental health during this life stage.
