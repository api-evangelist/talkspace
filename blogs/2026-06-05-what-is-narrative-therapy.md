This article explains narrative therapy, a collaborative approach that helps individuals separate themselves from their problems by examining the stories they tell about their lives. The piece covers how therapists use techniques such as externalization, re-authoring, and witnessing to help clients reframe dominant narratives that cause distress and develop more empowering personal stories. It also outlines which mental health conditions narrative therapy can help address and how to find a practitioner.
